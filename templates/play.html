Temporal Reasoning Task

In this game you will see several rounds where buttons (cues) appear.

Each button will reveal a color when clicked.

Your task is to determine which cue contains a bias towards RED.

Some cues might be occluded in the game which will force you to rely on the cues that are visible.

In the final round you'll be asked which one was the biased cue.
